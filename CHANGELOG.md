## [0.1.0a0] - 2026-08-14

### Added

- Strict ELScript 1.0 YAML loading, multi-file logical merge, validation, layered
  configuration, and persistent performance-state compilation.
- Provider-neutral planning plus deterministic fake and production ElevenLabs adapters.
- Safe single, scene, and segment audio output with privacy-aware manifests.
- Ordered synchronous/asynchronous streaming with attributed speech and timeline events.
- Content-addressed provider-result caching with continuity-aware invalidation.
- Stable diagnostics, coded warnings, secret redaction, atomic publication, and
  adversarial failure/security coverage.
- Public reference documentation, package verification, and cross-platform CI/release
  guidance.
